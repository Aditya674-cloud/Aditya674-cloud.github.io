01 / Profile

About my work

A mechanical engineering perspective on the design, analysis and development of intelligent physical systems.

From mechanical fundamentals to autonomous machines.

My interests span mechanical design, structural and thermal analysis, robotics, UAV systems and control. I enjoy understanding a system from first principles, translating requirements into engineering decisions, and documenting the reasoning behind each iteration.

I am currently developing my robotics and simulation workflow alongside project work in mechanical design and computational analysis. I value transparent engineering: clear assumptions, traceable calculations, honest progress reporting and validation before claims.

Current milestoneSelected for an upcoming ISAE Supméca / Euler Laboratory internship focused on a drone arena and Guidance, Navigation & Control (GNC). The work is an opportunity to deepen practical experience in autonomous navigation and obstacle-avoidance research.

I am interested in research environments where mechanical engineering, robotics, simulation and experimentation meet.
